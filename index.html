01 • Hair restoration

FUE Hair Transplantation

FUE is a minimally invasive surgical hair-restoration technique, not a completely non-surgical procedure.

How it works

  • Assess donor density, scalp condition and recipient area.
  • Extract individual follicular units with specialised instruments.
  • Prepare and place grafts according to the planned hairline.
  • Growth develops gradually over the following months.

Pre-care

  • Share medicines, supplements, allergies and medical history.
  • Follow the clinic's washing and medication instructions.
  • Avoid smoking/alcohol when advised around the procedure.
  • Do not apply unapproved scalp products before treatment.

Post-care & safety

  • Follow washing and sleeping instructions.
  • Protect the recipient area from friction and sun.
  • Do not pick crusts or scratch the scalp.
  • Contact the clinic for fever, pus, spreading redness or unusual bleeding.

02 • Hair therapy

PRP for Hair

PRP uses a patient's own blood, processed to concentrate platelets, then applied/injected according to the clinical protocol.

What to expect

  • Blood is collected and processed.
  • Prepared platelet-rich material is applied to selected scalp areas.
  • Multiple sessions may be recommended depending on the treatment plan.

Safety & aftercare

  • Tell the clinician about medicines and blood-related conditions.
  • Mild tenderness or swelling may occur.
  • Follow washing and exercise instructions.
  • Seek review for severe pain or infection signs.

04 • Hair therapy

LLLT for Hair Fall

Low-level light therapy is a non-invasive option used in selected hair-loss management programs.

Safety instructions

  • Use the device exactly as directed.
  • Discuss photosensitising medicines or conditions.
  • Do not use over untreated lesions where contraindicated.
  • Stop and seek advice for significant irritation.

Diet support

  • Prioritise adequate protein and varied whole foods.
  • Investigate suspected deficiencies with professional testing.
  • Avoid extreme crash diets.
05 • Scalp health

Dandruff Treatment

Flaking can have different causes, so diagnosis helps select the appropriate scalp-care plan.

How to reduce dandruff

  • Wash regularly according to scalp type.
  • Use an appropriate anti-dandruff shampoo when advised.
  • Avoid scratching and sharing combs or towels.
  • Reduce heavy product buildup.

Safety

  • Persistent redness, pain, crusting or hair loss needs review.
  • Avoid harsh home remedies on irritated skin.

07 • Resurfacing

Chemical Peel for Acne & Pigmentation

A controlled chemical exfoliation selected according to the skin concern, skin type and treatment depth.

Pre-care

  • Follow instructions about retinoids and exfoliants.
  • Disclose medicines and previous reactions.
  • Do not treat sunburned or severely irritated skin.

Post-care

  • Use broad-spectrum sunscreen and gentle moisturiser.
  • Do not pick peeling skin.
  • Pause unapproved active products until recovery.
  • Report blistering, severe pain or swelling.
08 • Skin wellness

Medi-Facial

Protocols can be tailored for acne, hyperpigmentation, back acne and skin glow goals.

Safety

  • Choose products according to sensitivity and diagnosis.
  • Avoid aggressive exfoliation on irritated skin.
  • Maintain sunscreen use.
  • Tell the provider about allergies and medications.

Diet support

  • Eat varied fruits and vegetables.
  • Choose adequate protein and healthy fats.
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Limit foods that you know worsen your individual symptoms.

11 • Skin tightening

Radio Frequency for Face

Controlled radio-frequency energy is used in selected skin-tightening and texture protocols.

Potential uses

  • Appearance of mild skin laxity.
  • Texture-focused treatment plans.
  • Gradual improvement may require multiple sessions.

Safety precautions

  • Tell the provider about implants and active skin disease.
  • Follow device-specific pre/post-care.
  • Report excessive heat or pain immediately.
  • Use sun protection after treatment.
12 • Scar management

Acne Scar Treatment

Scar type, skin tone and active acne are assessed before selecting a treatment approach.

Treatment planning

  • Assess scar type and active acne.
  • Select an appropriate procedure or combination where indicated.
  • Set realistic expectations; improvement is usually gradual.

Safety precautions

  • Do not pick active acne or healing areas.
  • Use sun protection to reduce pigment-change risk.
  • Follow wound-care instructions after resurfacing.
  • Report infection, severe swelling or unexpected pigment change.
